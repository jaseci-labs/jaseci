- **A comptime fold keys on the content it folded from**: a module that folds a value out of another module at compile time now records the producers its evaluation actually read (comptime bindings, interpreted bodies, enum member values, class-body constants, `has` defaults and embedded files, transitively) and invalidates when their content moves, instead of serving the value baked in by an earlier compile; unsealed precompiled bundles honor the same check, and producers are ingested with full trees so a fold no longer fails with `E0033` once the producer is served from its cache. Editing a module whose body a fold interpreted now costs a rebuild of the folding module on the next invocation, so a `comptime import` of a large module is a heavier dependency than a plain one.
