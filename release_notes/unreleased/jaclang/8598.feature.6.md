- **Grants can name a group, with membership as an edge**: `allow_root` writes one entry per grantee onto the granted object, so sharing with a large audience meant an ACL dict that grew with the audience -- re-serialised on every write and deserialised on every read of that object. `allow_group(obj, group_id, level)` grants to a group instead: one entry on the object, and one membership edge per member. `check_access_level` resolves a caller's groups from its outgoing edges and takes the highest level any of them grants, so an existing per-root grant keeps working unchanged and the two compose.
