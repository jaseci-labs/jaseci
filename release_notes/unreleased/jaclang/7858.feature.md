- **Feature: Borrows split at single-field granularity**: a borrow taken as exactly one attribute level on a root name (`&p.name`, `&mut p.left`) records the field it covers, so borrows and writes touching provably disjoint fields of one owner now coexist instead of tripping E1302/E1303. Whole-object borrows, same-field overlaps, subscripts, and paths deeper than one level conflict exactly as before, and a field borrow still pins its whole owner for destruction (E1304), escape (E1306), and sendability (E1308) checks.
