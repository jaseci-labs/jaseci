- **Native `pathlib`, `fnmatch`, `logging` and `contextvars`, plus the `os.path` gaps**: the native pathway gains a bundled `pathlib.Path` over the existing `os.path` floor (`Path(str)`/`Path(Path)`, `str()` and f-string interpolation, truthiness, `.name`, `.stem`, `.parent`, `/`, `.resolve()`, `.exists()`, `.is_dir()`), which is what `compiler/type_system/types.jac` needed to lower at all, along with `fnmatch`/`fnmatchcase`, a stderr `logging` surface, and `contextvars.ContextVar`. `import os` also reaches `abspath`, `splitext`, `relpath`, `normcase` and `getmtime`, and `os.sep` now resolves as a module attribute. Each shim is pinned by a test that runs one fixture through both the sv and na pathways and requires identical output, so CPython is the oracle; a member outside a shim's provided surface fails the native compile naming that member rather than silently answering wrong.
