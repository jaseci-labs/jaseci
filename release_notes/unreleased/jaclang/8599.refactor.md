- **Tests: the fixture-per-assertion and one-test-per-symbol suites collapse into tables.** The corpus was not redundant by content -- near-identical fixtures were 3% of it and outright duplicate tests 0.2% -- but by granularity: 58% of `.jac` fixtures fed exactly one assertion, and 1,856 test blocks were hand-written rows of tables nobody had written.
