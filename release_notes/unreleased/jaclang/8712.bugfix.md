- **Fix: the native backend's state fields say what they hold**: Twenty-eight fields on `NaIRGenState` were declared `object` or `dict[str, object]` while actually holding llvm IR handles and AST nodes, so reading one back was an attribute access on `object`. The osp and exception helpers hold `ir.Function`, the descriptor and kernel-init globals hold `ir.GlobalVariable`, the file, bytes and iterator struct fields hold `ir.Type`, and `arch_ast_map` holds `uni.Archetype`. Each is now named for what its assignments put in it. Four narrower ones go with it: the clib callback coercion and trampoline take the `ir.PointerType` their only caller already proves they get rather than a bare `ir.Type`, the struct pack and unpack helpers take the `Sequence[Expr | KWPair]` they are always handed rather than a bare `list`, `hash_core` narrows to `ir.IntType` before reading a type's `width`, and struct unpack emits the refcount helpers before reaching for the allocator. No runtime behaviour changes.
