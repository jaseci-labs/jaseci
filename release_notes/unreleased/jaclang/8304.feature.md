- **Scale: revision based rollback and a bundle retention contract**: a deploy already leaves a complete rollback target in the cluster, since the pod image is constant across versions and deploy patches rather than recreates, but nothing exposed or protected it. `revision_history_limit` is now a declared `KubernetesConfig` field that lands on every Deployment, defaulting to the 10 the API already applied, so rollback depth is something a consumer can read back instead of inferring. `list_revisions` and `rollback_to` on the Kubernetes target expose the retained revisions and revert to one. `bundle_keys_from_pod_spec` and `referenced_bundle_keys` give consumers the retention contract that a bundle referenced by a retained revision is never deleted, reading the keys back out of the init container the injector itself builds so the writer and the reader cannot drift.
