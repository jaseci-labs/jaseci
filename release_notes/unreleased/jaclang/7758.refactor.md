- **cl->na interop unified through the manifest**: the client/native wasm channel now flows through `BoundaryAnalysisPass` like every other cross-codespace crossing. `na`-marked and inferred client-native edge imports record typed `InteropBinding`s (export name, param/ret types, route, source module) in the interop manifest; the client build derives its wasm emission list from the new `client_native_imports` getter, and the `ClientManifest.na_modules` side-channel is removed. The E5083 stem-collision diagnostic moved to the boundary pass, wasm stem logic is deduped into `codeinfo.native_wasm_stem`, and the jir interop cache now round-trips `kind` and `route` so cached walker bindings keep their spawn semantics.
