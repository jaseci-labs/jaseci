- **Fix: concurrent replica boot against a fresh database**: `ensure_schema` serialized its DDL with a process-local lock, but the tables, types and indexes it creates belong to the database every replica shares, so two replicas starting together on an empty database raced and the loser died with `23505 duplicate key` on the Postgres catalog. The critical section is now a session-level `pg_advisory_lock` keyed on the schema namespace, with the schema stamp re-read under the lock; `23505`, `42P07` and `42710` are additionally treated as "a concurrent creator won" and re-check the stamp instead of raising.
