- **Fix: only one caller now probes a recovering service-to-service dependency**: when the circuit breaker's cooldown elapsed, every caller that arrived was let through at once, so a dependency that had just been declared unhealthy took the full concurrent burst instead of a single trial call. One caller now carries the trial while the rest keep failing fast, and a trial that ends without a result hands the breaker back instead of leaving it stuck shedding every call. A reply that arrives after its trial has already been given up on is ignored, so a slow dependency cannot reopen itself to full traffic while a newer trial is still in flight.
