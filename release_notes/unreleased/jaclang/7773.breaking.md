- **Breaking: sv/cl/na placement markers removed**: Placement is fully inferred; `[placement.pins]` in jac.toml is the override escape hatch and `[scale.microservices.routes]` is the authoritative microservice cut (`jac scale split <module>` writes an entry, and imports of cut members lower to service RPC automatically). Run `jac fix placement` to migrate existing projects -- it strips markers, renames the retired `.sv.jac`/`.cl.jac`/`.na.jac` files, preserves service topology into the routes table, and pins any element whose inferred placement differs from its old marker. No filename spells a codespace: placement is inferred, pinned, or forced, and a module-level `client`/`native` pin coerces the whole module the way the old suffix did. The placement lockfile introduced by #7757 (`jac check --update-placements-lock` / `jac.placements.lock`) is removed before release: placement is the compiler's business, reviewed on demand with `jac check --placements`, never snapshotted.
