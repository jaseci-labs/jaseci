- **Breaking: sv/cl/na placement markers removed**: Placement is fully inferred; `[placement.pins]` in jac.toml is the override escape hatch and `[scale.microservices.routes]` is the authoritative microservice cut (`jac scale split <module>` writes an entry, and imports of cut members lower to service RPC automatically). Run `jac fix placement` to migrate existing projects -- it strips markers, renames `.sv.jac` files, preserves service topology into the routes table, and pins any element whose inferred placement differs from its old marker. Variant modules (`.cl.jac` / `.na.jac`) now declare their space with a `variant client;` / `variant native;` directive. The placement lockfile introduced by #7757 (`jac check --update-placements-lock` / `jac.placements.lock`) is removed before release: placement is the compiler's business, reviewed on demand with `jac check --placements`, never snapshotted.
