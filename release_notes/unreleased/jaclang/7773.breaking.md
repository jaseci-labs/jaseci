The `sv` / `cl` / `na` placement markers, statement prefixes, and the `.sv.jac` suffix are removed: placement is fully inferred, with `[placement.pins]` in jac.toml as the override escape hatch and `[scale.microservices.routes]` as the authoritative microservice cut (`jac scale split <module>` writes an entry; imports of cut members lower to service RPC automatically). Run `jac fix placement` to migrate existing projects mechanically -- it strips markers, renames `.sv.jac` files, preserves service topology into the routes table, and pins any element whose inferred placement differs from its old marker. Variant modules (`.cl.jac` / `.na.jac`) now declare their space with a `variant client;` / `variant native;` directive (declaration-first, suffix as compatibility fallback), and `jac check --update/--verify-placements-lock` makes placement drift reviewable and CI-enforceable.
