- **Breaking: Mongo/Redis/Firestore/SQLAlchemy scaffolding removed**: The remaining affordances from the pre-0.36 storage stack are gone. `[eject.db]` legacy drivers (`sqlite`/`sql`/`sqlalchemy`) now fail at eject time with a diagnostic (the runtime persists natively to Postgres; set `JAC_DB_URL` or rely on the embedded server). `[scale.websocket].backplane` accepts `memory` or `pg` only. The sqlite identity fallback is removed: serving without a reachable database fails loudly instead of silently opening a second credential store. The `data.mongo`/`data.redis`/`data.sql` capabilities and their intent rules are removed, so served apps no longer close over `pymongo`/`sqlalchemy`.
- **Fix: scheduler and ops defects from the clean break**: `/jobs` endpoints return 503 `STORAGE_UNAVAILABLE` on Postgres outages (the branch previously tested the unraisable `PyMongoError` and always fell through to 500), the admin ops page renders the Postgres health card (it iterated a stale mongo/redis signal list), webhook API-key lookups are indexed, and every `[scale.*]` section read by the config loader is schema-declared.
