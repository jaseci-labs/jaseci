- **Fix: spurious type errors and crashes on cyclic or deeply nested imports**: while the type checker was resolving a symbol that referred back into its own in-progress resolution (mutually referencing modules or types), the temporarily unresolved type could surface as false "cannot access attribute" and type-mismatch diagnostics, and a legitimately deep (non-cyclic) import graph could overrun the recursion limit and crash. Such in-flight types are now recognized and left to settle once resolution unwinds, and deep import closures compile without error.
