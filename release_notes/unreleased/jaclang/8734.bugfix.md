- **Fix: KEDA scale-to-zero wake latency**: A woken scale-to-zero pod paid up to ~44s before answering its first request, mostly from re-running `jac install` against an empty venv on every boot plus coarse startup/readiness probe polling. `jac install` now skips reinstalling when the venv already matches the resolved dependency hash, restored from a dependency-hash-keyed cache on the bundle PVC that survives the pod's `emptyDir` wipe across restarts; the startup probe's period is now configurable (default 2s, was a hardcoded 10s) and the readiness probe tightens the same way for services with a replica floor of 0, both scaling their failure thresholds to keep the original total budget.
