- **Fix: a local first-assigned inside an effect lambda kept its `let` again instead of blanking the client**: since #7925, client codegen queued the hoisted `let` for a lambda-local on the effect's scope but `_transform_to_useeffect` never drained the queue - the declaration was silently dropped, the assignment emitted bare, and the strict-mode bundle threw `ReferenceError` at boot, unmounting the whole client to its error boundary. Effect bodies now drain pending hoists like every other function body, and a new E5085 internal codegen error fails the compile loudly if any scope ever pops with undrained hoisted declarations again.
