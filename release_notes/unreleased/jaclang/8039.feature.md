- The parser constructs typed `unitree` nodes directly (issue #8039, Track A): the syntax arena, its materializer, and the arena's comptime-derived builder surface are deleted, so `frontend.parse` hands back a `Module` with no marshalling bridge and comptime expansion runs on the object tree. Front-end parse-to-tree time drops roughly 2x on CPython because the arena-to-objects materialization step no longer exists. Where the sealed native parser artifact is active it serves these parses directly: a 44 KB module parses in about 16 ms and a 233 KB one in about 77 ms, roughly 10x and 14x under the old arena path. On the bytecode fallback one accepted regression remains until the front-end passes are native: `jac check --parse_only` and LSP syntax pre-publish diagnostics come from a full object parse (~89 ms on a 44 KB module, up from ~9 ms), since there is no longer an arena to stop at.
- **Loop-narrowing regression guards**: the checker's sound loop-body narrowing (a loop body that reassigns a narrowed symbol now sees the conservative back-edge merge at the top of the body, not the iteration-one view) is pinned by its own test, which asserts diagnosed locations rather than a count and holds the line on the two shapes that must stay clean -- a condition that re-narrows on every iteration, and a body that never writes the symbol. The nested same-kind back-edge guard it had begun to overlap no longer widens the narrowed symbol inside the loop, so it isolates back-edge detection on its own again.
- The parser and `unitree` seal together (issue #8039, Track A0/A2): `unitree.jac` joins `NATIVE_SEAL_MODULES`, its whole native closure lowers with only four enumerated waivers, and the sealed artifact passes a six-corpus parity gate against the bytecode parser -- equal error counts, node counts, and tree fingerprints on the canary corpus, `fam.jac`, a real compiler source module, jsx backtracking, and non-ascii sources.
