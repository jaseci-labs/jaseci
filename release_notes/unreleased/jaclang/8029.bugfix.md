- **Fix: the sv-to-sv circuit breaker now trips after the number of failures it advertises**: a failed service-to-service call was counted as a single breaker failure no matter how many times it had retried on the wire, so the breaker took roughly three times its configured threshold of failed round trips before it started shedding load. Failures are now counted per round trip, and a call stops retrying as soon as the breaker opens, so a recovery probe costs one round trip instead of three.
