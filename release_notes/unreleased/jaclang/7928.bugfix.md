- **Fix: narrowing died on loop fall-through edges, breaking `jac check` on main**: the flow walk types a loop header under its loop-ENTRY state (back-edges are skipped), and after #7852 wired the missing loop-exit edges, consumers past the loop inherited that entry state as if the body never ran - so an `Optional` initialized to `None` and assigned inside the loop was reported as definitely `NoneType`, an `is not None` guard after the loop narrowed it to `Never`, and the guard body lost narrowing entirely (the `ts_decl_parser.jac:1155` E1001 that kept main's full-repo jac-check red). The walk now reports unnarrowed on a loop's fall-through edge whenever an iteration can rewrite the target (assignment targets collected across the body, nested closures included), falling back to the declared type there, while loop bodies keep their entry-state typing.
