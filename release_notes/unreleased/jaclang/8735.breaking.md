- **Breaking: the fused native compiler seal is removed**: The `jac` binary no longer builds or ships a `libjac_compiler` native artifact; the compiler runs from its precompiled JIR image, and the image manifest moves to format 8 without a `native` record (older app images stay loadable). The hidden `jac seal` command and the `jac check --compile-stats` flag are removed. User-program native compilation (`jac nacompile`, `jac build --native`) is unchanged. The compiler modules served natively are now listed explicitly in `jaclang/compiler/native_scope.jac` (empty for now); tracking in #8732.
