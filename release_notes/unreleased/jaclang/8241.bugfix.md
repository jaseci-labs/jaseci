Optional containers keep their element layout in the native lane. A `T | None`
slot lowers to a presence tag beside the container pointer, and every loop,
comprehension, slice and index over one used to hand that wrapper to the
container helpers, crashing the emitter and costing the whole callable its
lowering. The payload is now unwrapped at each consuming head, and an absent
optional raises the same `TypeError` CPython raises rather than reading a null.
`x or []` and `x or {}` read their element layout from the operand beside them
instead of guessing and erasing both branches to an untyped pointer.
