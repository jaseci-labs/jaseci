- **Fix: stub symbols stop anchoring dead programs**: The type evaluator shares parsed `.pyi` stub modules across every `JacProgram` in a process, and `Symbol.add_use` recorded use-sites on their symbols, so one compile's builtin references kept that whole program reachable for the process lifetime -- ASTs, inference results, and for native compiles the LLVM artifacts. Long-lived `jac test` workers and full-repo `jac check` runs grew until the box died. Stub-owned symbols now bind `nd.sym` without recording the use-site, repeat compiles hold flat memory, and `tests/compiler/test_stub_use_retention.jac` pins the behavior from both sides.
