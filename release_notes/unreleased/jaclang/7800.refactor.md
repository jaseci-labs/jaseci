The unitree drops `ast`, `typing`, `copy`, and `builtins`: read/write context tagging is now the native `ExprCtx` enum (pyast_gen maps it back to `ast3.expr_context` at its own seam, and four analysis passes lose their `import ast` entirely), `String.lit_value` decodes escapes with a self-hosted decoder instead of `ast3.literal_eval`, and `sem_token`'s `dir(builtins)` probe becomes the static `BUILTIN_CALLABLE_NAMES` frozenset. parser.jac's host-interop glue -- the sealed-lexer ctypes bind, bytecode-lexer fallback, diag-to-Alert conversion, comment attachment, and jac:ignore scanning -- moves to a new bytecode-tier `parser/frontend.jac`; the package still exports `parse`, so callers are unchanged. Native census re-rank: parser.jac's top blocker goes from the ctypes shim to transitive `pathlib`; unitree's from `builtins` to `collections.abc`.
