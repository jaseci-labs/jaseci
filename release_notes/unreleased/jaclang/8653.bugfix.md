- **Fix: test pool workers retire on memory, not just on files served**: A worker only retired after `JAC_TEST_RECYCLE` files (default 25), so on compile-heavy suites the pool grew without bound within a single recycle window and exhausted small machines regardless of `JAC_TEST_JOBS`. Workers now also retire once their peak RSS crosses `JAC_TEST_WORKER_MAX_RSS_MB` (1536 by default, `0` to disable), and `auto` budgets the measured per-worker peak so it stops over-subscribing.
