- **Serving: gzip negotiation in the transport (#8903)**: Every server jaclang runs (`jac serve`, scale services, the microservice gateway, and the embedded gateway) now answers `Accept-Encoding: gzip` with a gzipped body for text, JSON, JavaScript, SVG, XML, and wasm responses of 1 KB or more, and compresses streamed bodies (SSE, proxied responses) incrementally so frames still arrive as they are produced. The decision is made once, in `ServingServer._write_response`, as a policy step over the resolved body; `Vary: Accept-Encoding` is merged onto every negotiable response, a handler's own `Content-Length` is replaced rather than doubled, and a body that already carries `Content-Encoding` passes through untouched, so a service's gzip flows through the gateway without being compressed twice. Configure it under `[serve.compression]` (`enabled`, `min_bytes`, `level`, default on at level 4). The gateway's `raw_forward` now asks upstreams for identity, because it parses those bodies itself: without that, a compressing service's nodes silently vanished from `/graph/data`. Token-issuing responses (`/user/login`, `/user/refresh-token`) carry `Cache-Control: no-store, no-transform` and are never compressed. The ingress-nginx `gzip-types` list is generated from the same media-type table and now includes `text/javascript`, which is what the client bundle is served as.
