- **Ordering is written where the filter is written**: A traversal can now carry an ordering term alongside its predicates, in the position that already encodes which side it reads -- `[chan ->:Posted:-sent:-> [?:Msg]]` orders by the carrying edge's field, `[chan ->:Posted:-> [?:Msg, at > 3, -at]]` filters and orders on the node. A term is a bare field name (ascending) or its negation (descending); a predicate always carries a comparison operator, so the two never collide and no grammar change was needed. This is the only way a traversal can order by an edge field, since the result list holds nodes. Ordering terms must follow every predicate, and ordering on a non-final hop is refused rather than silently mis-scoped.
