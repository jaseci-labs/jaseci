- **Breaking: The ambient permission constants are replaced by an ambient `AccessLevel` enum**: `NoPerm` / `ReadPerm` / `ConnectPerm` / `WritePerm` are removed. The `AccessLevel` enum they aliased is now itself ambient (no import) and its members are the one spelling of access levels everywhere a level is taken: `grant(node, level=AccessLevel.READ)`, `Jac.allow_root(node, rid, AccessLevel.WRITE)`, `return AccessLevel.WRITE;` in `__jac_access__`. The mapping is 1:1 (`NoPerm`→`AccessLevel.NO_ACCESS`, `ReadPerm`→`AccessLevel.READ`, `ConnectPerm`→`AccessLevel.CONNECT`, `WritePerm`→`AccessLevel.WRITE`), and `def __jac_access__ -> AccessLevel` now type-checks (the constants were stub-typed `int`, which rejected the honest signature). See [Breaking Changes](../breaking-changes.md).
