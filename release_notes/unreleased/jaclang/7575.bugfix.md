- **Native kernel glob-init symbols come from one minter**: every glob-init emission, link, and rename site (the entry codegen, OSP setup, the osp/fmt kernel link gates in `na_compile_pass`, and dependency renames) now mints its symbol through `native_glob_init_name`/`native_glob_init_keyed_name` in `codeinfo.jac` instead of hand-building the `__jac_glob_init_` prefix. This fixes #6870 for both the OSP and fmt kernels: the sentinel sources stay hash-free even when other transitive deps carry hashed keys, and manifest `native_transitive_sources`/`native_transitive_keys` stay index-aligned when a kernel is registered mid-walk.
