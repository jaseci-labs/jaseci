- **Feature: jac-serverless**: `jac start --serverless` runs any Jac app statelessly with its graph and users in a managed Postgres (e.g. Supabase); every walker doubles as a serverless function, with an optional `jac serverless` command for local invocation, Lambda packaging with a prebuilt client, and per-walker deploys. Also hardens cold-cache serve compiles (inference gating, native demote on early scheduling crashes, non-fatal missing-analysis passes outside aot builds).
